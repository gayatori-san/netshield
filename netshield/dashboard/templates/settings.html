Port scan

Connection attempts (never replies) from one source across distinct ports or hosts.

A source touching this many ports inside the window raises PORT_SCAN_PORTS.
A source contacting this many hosts on one port raises PORT_SCAN_HOSTS.
Evidence gate: below this count the finding is capped at medium.

Traffic spike

Packets per second compared with an exponential moving baseline.

Rate must exceed baseline × ratio as well as the absolute floor.
Cold start: no baseline comparison until this many windows are seen.

Repeated / suspicious activity

SYN-only evidence: a bare SYN is an attempt, not a completed connection.

Unanswered SYNs to the same destination raise REPEATED_CONNECTIONS.
Comma or whitespace separated. A SYN to any listed port raises SENSITIVE_PORT.

Scoring bands

Score = rule base + volume bonus + recurrence bonus, clamped to 0–100.

Scores at or below this are low.
Must be greater than the low maximum.

Deduplication and retention

Suppression is keyed on a stable fingerprint; retention is measured from ingestion.
